# *Kei* - Productivity Agent

## Motto

> *Kei: You cannot change what happened, but you can influence what you are about to do.*

## Problem

Productivity is a thing that as less maintainance as possible, but being there to support when you feel down or low. When you're alone, you are most likely to fell into the trap of working 'busy-work' or do something that you do not truly want. It does not matter whether you are alone or you are in social structure that rules you. If social structure rules you, you lose ability to choose. Another reason why it should be as frictionless as possible is because if productivity tool creates friction, then it will be avoided unless motivation is high, which contradicts the reason of using this tool.

For every day actions, it also would be more useful to have notes to write, since such single moment actions like selling an item are great identity assets and in process they need for example the minimal selling price, otherwise you might not sell or might end up unsatisfied. Therefore having notes that are adaptive for goal can turn results and boost motivation.

Another example is the problem that when, for example, you go to hang-out, you don't decide on how much it will take unless you are restricted by external factors. It would be beneficial to have a 'calendar' that shows "strict timeline" or "allowed range". Mainly, because for humans it is hard to predict, that it why in business there is a gap of 20% or more percent of money or time for execution for trade.

## Mission

To empower intentional living by turning every moment into an opportunity for conscious choice.

## Values

- *Effortless Agency:* The tool makes it easy to do what you truly want to do, removing the friction between thought and action.

- *Graceful Adaptation:* Moving with the world, not against it. When states change, the plan evolves without guilt.

- *Directional Truth:* Success is staying true to your mission. We prioritize the "Why" behind every task.

- Limited number of tasks a day. 6-7, then show danger zone...
- Support for future you via making decision making simpler and more conscious.
- Minimum, maximum and ideal, average (analyzed based on observation) range of activity.
- &? Breaking down task into subtasks using user data.

- Transition action generation, storage and improvement. && Small Win Actions for transition. Transitions should be related and help to do the next top-level action. In order to lower possibility of friction decision making.
- Habit reflection to perform self-analysis of usefulness of a habit, and gaps for improvement.
- Take into account priority, need, joy and other metadatas of an action.
- Habits vault where person's habits get to drop into after number of times person has followed the habit without an issue. It will occasionally show the person the habit, and ask the questions like if this habit is still useful, or if it wills good and seemless, or creates friction. If he wants to find an opportunity to find better way to do that? Lack of self-awareness is a poison. Reflection and analysis are the antidote. That is why to be more productive, you have to reflect on old habits to find out if they are making any good and if they can be improved and benefit of improvement overweights losses.
- Activity archive, where all events are being dropped to follow changelog and analyze long-term performance.
