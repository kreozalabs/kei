# Kei User Guide: Managing Your Tasks and Schedule

Kei makes it easy to organize, prioritize, and reschedule your daily tasks. This guide explains how to use these features to keep your lists neat and focused.

---

## 1. Organizing Your Tasks (Reordering)

You can arrange your tasks on any day in the exact order you want them to appear.

### Move Tasks with Numbers (Fast Position Edit)
If you have a long list and want to move a task to a specific spot (for example, moving the 7th task to the 2nd spot):
1. **Find the number** on the left side of the task.
2. **Click on the number** to edit it.
3. Type the new number where you want the task to go (e.g., `2`) and press `Enter` on your keyboard (or click anywhere else).
4. The task will instantly slide to its new position, and all other task numbers will adjust automatically.

### Move to the Very Top or Very Bottom (Shift + Chevron Click)
If you want to move a task to the absolute top or absolute bottom of your day quickly:
- **Move to the absolute top:** Hold down the `Shift` key on your keyboard and click the **Up Arrow** (`▲`) next to the task.
- **Move to the absolute bottom:** Hold down the `Shift` key on your keyboard and click the **Down Arrow** (`▼`) next to the task.

---

## 2. Choosing Where New Tasks Go

When you type a new task, you can decide whether it should go to the top of your list or the bottom.

### For a Single Task
In the task creation box, look at the bottom action bar. You will see a button labeled **Top** or **Bottom**:
- Click it to toggle the default position.
- If set to **Top**, your new task will appear at the very top of your day's list.
- If set to **Bottom**, it will appear at the very bottom.

### Set a Permanent Preference
If you always want new tasks to go to a specific place (for example, always starting at the top):
1. Go to the **Settings** page (usually in the side menu).
2. Scroll to the **Behavior** section.
3. Find **Default Task Position** and choose either **Top** or **Bottom**.
4. Now, whenever you open the creation box, it will default to your choice automatically.

---

## 3. Rescheduling Tasks in One Click

When you need to delay a task, you can reschedule it instantly with a single click instead of opening a calendar menu.

- **For Overdue Tasks:** Hover over any task that is in the red "Overdue" section and click the **Calendar icon** (`📅`). The task will instantly move to **Today**.
- **For Today's Tasks:** Hover over a task on today's list and click the **Calendar icon** (`📅`). It will move to **Tomorrow**.
- **For Future Tasks:** If a task is scheduled for tomorrow or later, the reschedule menu item will dynamically show the weekday name (for example, **"Reschedule to Tuesday"**). Clicking it will move the task to the day right after its current date.

*Note: If you reschedule a task by mistake, an **Undo** notification will pop up at the bottom of the screen. Click it to bring the task back to its original date and spot.*

---

## 4. Keeping Tasks in Order when Editing

When you edit a task's title or mark it as important, Kei remembers its position. The task will stay exactly where you put it in the list and will not jump to the bottom.

---

## 5. Rescheduling Multiple Tasks at Once

If you select multiple tasks using the check circles on the left, you can reschedule them all at once to a new day using the bulk action bar at the bottom:
- When they move to the new day, they will **keep their relative order** (the task that was higher up on your list will still be higher up on the new day).
- They will not get jumbled or crowd the bottom of the list.

### Selecting Tasks on Mobile
Since hover is not available on mobile devices, you can activate selection checkboxes by opening the header dropdown menu and selecting **Selection Mode**. Once active, checkboxes will be displayed next to all tasks.

---

## 6. Customizing Checkbox Visibility

By default, checkboxes appear dynamically when you hover over a task row. If you prefer a cleaner or more static interface:
1. Go to the **Settings** page.
2. Scroll to the **Behavior** section.
3. Under **Multi-Select & Bulk Actions**, find the **Checkbox Visibility** setting:
   - **On Hover:** Checkboxes appear when hovering over a task row on desktop for quick selection.
   - **Mode Only:** Checkboxes only appear when Selection Mode is explicitly turned on from the header dropdown menu, keeping your lists completely clean during daily navigation.

---

## 7. Previewing Tasks on Hover

You can quickly preview additional details of a task (like long descriptions, projects, and metadata) without opening the full edit view:
- **How to view:** Hover your mouse cursor over any task in your list.
- **Wikipedia-style interaction:** The preview popover stays open as long as your cursor remains over the task row or the popover itself, allowing you to read or interact with it comfortably.
- **Smooth exit:** Moving your mouse away from both the task and the popover will close it gracefully after a brief delay.
